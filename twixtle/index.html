How to Play Twixtle

Transform the START word into the END word in exactly 4 steps. Each of the 4 move types is used exactly once:

ANAGRAM
Rearrange the exact same letters to make a new word.
Example: ARC ➔ CAR, HEART ➔ EARTH
COMPOUND
Combine the word with another word to form a single compound word.
Example: CAR ➔ SIDE (sidecar), SWEAT ➔ BAND (sweatband)
HOMOPHONE
Swap for a word that sounds identical but is spelled differently.
Example: SIDE ➔ SIGHED, FLOWER ➔ FLOUR, BARE ➔ BEAR
VERB
Change to another grammatical tense or form of the same verb.
Example: SIGHED ➔ SIGH, RUN ➔ RAN, SING ➔ SINGING
★ PERFECT MODE: Keep all move pills unrevealed as ? and deduce the move types yourself to earn a Gold Star! Tapping a ? pill will reveal that move type as a hint.
